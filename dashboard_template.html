Importance formula

55% frequency (log-scaled relative to chapter), 25% sample-adjusted Black practical score and 20% engine evaluation from the repertoire side. +10 points for a practical trap: near-equal engine position with at least 40 games and an unusually high practical score. This is a study-priority heuristic, not an objective strength metric.

How to explore

The graph flows left to right: drag to pan, scroll to zoom. Click a node for position details and original move comments; double-click to explore its branch. +/− includes or excludes a subtree. The smaller bottom-right control expands or collapses an unbranched chain. Manual choices override automatic filtering.
